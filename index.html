How to read this

Coloured months show when a product is at its best, freshest, or traditionally made or released in Italy. Dried goods, cured meats and aged cheeses keep much longer, so their months mark the harvest, the curing season or the moment they are traditionally eaten.

Timing shifts by weeks between the Alps and Sicily, and by altitude and weather year to year. Region tags mark places with a notable tradition or protected origin, not the only places a product grows. Hunting and fishing seasons are set locally; check your regional calendario venatorio or ittico, and the fermo pesca (closed fishing periods), which differ between the Adriatic and Tyrrhenian seas.

DOP and IGP are EU protected designations. Presìdio marks a Slow Food Presidium protecting a small traditional production.
